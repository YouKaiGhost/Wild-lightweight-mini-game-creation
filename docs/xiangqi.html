Prototype notes

What this one is teaching me

The board drawing is also the rulebook.

01

The palace is a rule

Generals and advisors never leave the 3 × 3 palace. Elephants never cross the river. Once those constraints are in code, the board stops being decoration and starts rejecting moves.

02

No AI on purpose

Local two-player keeps v0.1 about legal moves, check, and checkmate. An engine can be its own prototype; mixing it into the first board would hide the rules bugs.
