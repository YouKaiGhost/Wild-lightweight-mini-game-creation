Prototype notes

What this one is teaching me

Two things I had to fix before the rally felt fair.

01

Fixed step first

Draw at the screen rate, simulate at 120 Hz. The ball behaves the same on a 60 Hz and a 144 Hz display, and small steps make tunnelling impossible at this speed.

02

Fair is not perfect

The CPU has a speed cap, a reaction delay, and a small aim error. Losing should feel like a mistake you can learn from, not a trick.
