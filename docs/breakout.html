Prototype notes

What this one is teaching me

Two rules that keep the wall from feeling random.

01

Bounce by the smaller overlap

The ball picks the axis with the smaller penetration, so a corner hit flips one direction instead of both. Previous-position checks handle clean face hits.

02

One wall is enough

Five rows and three lives make a complete game. More rows would make it longer, not better; power-ups belong to another prototype.
