Prototype notes

What this one is teaching me

Two special moves that are mostly edge cases.

01

Castling is three checks

The king cannot start in check, cross an attacked square, or land in check. The rook can be attacked; only the king's path matters. Rights disappear when the king or rook moves — or when the rook is captured on its home square.

02

En passant is a ghost

The target square exists for exactly one ply. The captured pawn is beside the destination, not on it, so it has to be removed explicitly. Clear the target after every move or the ghost survives one turn too long.
